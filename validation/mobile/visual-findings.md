# Mobile visual findings — targeted foundation pass

The first independent 375px render of the public home route showed that the headline wrapped at nearly every word and consumed too much of the initial viewport. The hero was adjusted to use a smaller mobile floor, the full content width, a 20px phone gutter, and a safe-area-aware top offset. A second capture taken before the splash timeout showed only the expected branded launch screen, confirming the dark splash background rather than validating final layout. A full 5-second capture confirmed the adjusted headline is readable, but it exposed an awkward one-word second line in the primary action. The phone action label was shortened to "Start the assessment" and the latest render confirmed it is a full-width, single-line, thumb-reachable primary control.
